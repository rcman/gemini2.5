write a 3d OPen-World in a third-person using html5 and three.min.js, all js files are local on the web server.  Players must manage hunger, health, and stamina while crafting, building their base from wildlife and hostile AI hunters. The game blends survival with base-building and resource management, providing a tense, immersive solo or co-op experience. The js files need to be split up for easy management. When pressing the key I to open and close the inventory which displays on the screen. There is also a lower quick-bar at the bottom on the screen with 8 slots. Right clicking on an item in the quick-slot moves it to inventory. Player spawns with an axe, pickaxe, knife and a canteen

Resources Found on the Map

- Wood – Harvested from trees using an axe. Essential for fuel, crafting, and building.
- Fiber – Found as smhas all plant nodes; used in cordage and clothing.
- Vegetables – Wild plants like blueberries, carrots, onions.
- Animals – Chickens, rabbits, deer, wolves, bears, cougars.
- Water – Gathered from lakes, boiled to purify.
- Medical Plants – Used for biofuel and first aid.

Different Ore & Mining Resources can be found on different rocks.

- Iron Ore – Most common; found on rocks.
- Copper Ore

Animal Products

- Fat – Rendered into biofuel.
- Leather – From animals, used in crafting clothes.
- Meat – Cooked for food, spoils if left too long.
- Feathers – For arrows and gear.

Tools & Survival Gear

- Axe – Wood and metal scrap.
- Pickaxe – Used to mine ores.
- Fishing Rod – Cordage, sticks, scrap.
- Bows & Arrows – Wood, fiber, nails, feathers.
- Campfire – For warmth and cooking.
- Bandages & First Aid Kits – Cloth, medicinal herbs.

A Workbench is a craftable item that the player can craft and place in the world. This workbench has its own build menu when accessing it with the letter E.

Items that can be make at a Creafting Table.

- Lockpicks – For locked crates; require iron fragments
- Rifle Rounds, 9mm, Shotgun Shells – Requires casings (from copper/zinc), gunpowder
- Rope – Sinew and cordage.
- nails
- rope

The Forge is craftable by the player and can be placed in the world. It can be accessed with the letter E and has ot's own build menu items

Place raw ore (Iron, Copper, zinc)
- Produces Iron, copper and zinc ingots

Player Building System. Items that can be built and placed in the world by the player. All items listed below snap to each other. Use the mouse wheel to rotate a building item for placement. A foundation needs to put down first in order to add walls to this building. You can remove an item by right clicking on it with your mouse.

- Base Foundation
- Walls
- Walls with Windows
- Ceilings, Stairs, Doors, Railings
- use B to open and close the build menu.

Building Materials

- Wood Planks – Made from logs.
- Nails – Found in loot crates or crafted from iron.
- Cordage – Made from fiber.
- Metal Scrap – Found in the environment.
