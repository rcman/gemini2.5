write a 3d OPen-World in a third-person using html5 and three.min.js, all js files are local on the web server. This open-world survival game set in a hostile wilderness environment. Players must manage hunger, health, and stamina while crafting, building, and defending their base from wildlife and hostile AI hunters. The game blends survival with base-building and resource management, providing a tense, immersive solo or co-op experience. The js files need to be split up for easy management. When pressing the key I to open and close the inventory which displays on the screen. There is also a lower quick-bar at the bottom on the screen with 8 slots. Right clicking on an item in the quick-slot moves it to inventory.

Resources Found on the Map

The world is rich with resources critical for survival and crafting. These include:
Natural Resources
- Wood – Harvested from trees using an axe. Essential for fuel, crafting, and building.
- Fiber – Found as small plant nodes; used in cordage and clothing.
- Vegetables – Wild plants like blueberries, carrots, onions.
- Animals – Chickens, rabbits (can be captured or hunted), deer, wolves, bears, cougars.
- Water – Gathered from lakes, boiled to purify.
- Medical Plants – Used for biofuel and first aid.

Ore & Mining Resources

- Iron Ore – Most common; found on rocks or in caves.
- Copper Ore – Common for electronics and casings.
- Potassium Ore – Used in fertilizer and gunpowder.
- Zinc Ore – Needed for alloy sheets and upgrades.
- Phosphorus – Found in caves; essential for high-end ammunition.
- Crystal (Premium Crystals) – Rare; found in deep caves for advanced upgrades.
- Coal – Also found in caves; used for fuel.

Animal Products

- Fat – Rendered into biofuel.
- Leather – From animals, used in crafting clothes.
- Meat – Cooked for food, spoils if left too long.
- Feathers – For arrows and gear.
- Antlers – From deer; used in adhesive.

Crafting Overview

Crafting is split into:

Basic Inventory Craftin

Workbench Crafting

- Fabricator, Stove, Refinery, and Forge Crafting

Craftable Items in the Player Inventory

Tools & Survival Gear

- Axe – Wood and metal scrap.
- Pickaxe – Used to mine ores.
- Fishing Rod – Cordage, sticks, scrap.
- Bows & Arrows – Wood, fiber, nails, feathers.
- Campfire – For warmth and cooking.
- Bandages & First Aid Kits – Cloth, medicinal herbs.

The Workbench allows advanced crafting:

Key Items

- Lockpicks – For locked crates; require iron fragments and power/mass.
- Gunpowder – Potassium and ash.
- Rifle Rounds, 9mm, Shotgun Shells – Requires casings (from copper/zinc), gunpowder.
- Circuit Boards – Copper, pearls, electronics.
- Rope – Sinew and cordage.
- Adhesive – Ash, antlers, medicinal plants.Alloy Sheets – Zinc and iron fragments.

Forge System

Place raw ore (Iron, Copper, zinc)

- Produces Iron Fragments, Copper Fragments, etc.
- Crystal Forge (for premium crystal) is accessed in deep caves.

Building System

Core Component

- Base Foundation
- Walls (with or without windows)
- Ceilings, Stairs, Doors, Railings
- Glass Walls and Ceilings (later tech)

Building Materials

- Wood Planks – Made from logs.
- Nails – Found in loot crates or crafted from iron.
- Cordage – Made from fiber.
- Metal Scrap – Found in the environment.

Building Mechanics

Use "B" key to open the building menu.

- Snap and rotate pieces for precision placement.
- Can build multi-level structures, greenhouses, and fencing.
- Upgrade walls with alloy sheets to strengthen against attacks.
